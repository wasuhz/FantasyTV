Keep the SVG files as editable scalable originals. App and store exports use
full square images; platforms apply their own icon masking. Android adaptive
icons may require foreground/background layers and platform-specific safe zone
adjustments when the final app is assembled.

This pack is a clean vector recreation of the approved visual concept,
optimized for consistent sizes and crisp delivery.
