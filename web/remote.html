The game on the TV shows no score or points here, since your phone is ahead of the stream. The game on screen moves left (with its sound), the other plays muted on the right, until it cools off. Uses a second stream from your IPTV. The ⧉ button on a game card splits with it yourself.
Any play that gains one of your starters something (yards, a catch, a touchdown, a kick), in any game: "7 YDS · B. Robinson · 7-yd run". ESPN reports it seconds after the play, usually before your stream shows it. ESPN reports plays before your stream shows them. When one of your starters makes a big play (touchdown, 20+ yards) in another game, the TV switches there in time to watch it. Needs the stream delay right (use Sync below).
Stream delay: how far your IPTV runs behind live. The TV waits this long before reacting, so it never spoils a play. Type the clock you see on the TV for the game playing; the TV works out the delay and saves it.
